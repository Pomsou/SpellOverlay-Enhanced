SpellOverlay Enhanced is a lightweight yet powerful tool that gives you complete control over Blizzard's built-in Spell Activation Overlays (procs). Whether you want to resize, recolor, move, or add a dynamic pulse effect to your procs.


**Features**

+ Pulse Effect: Add a "heartbeat" glow to your procs with customizable speed and minimum intensity.
+ Complete Visual Control: Adjust Scale, Alpha (Opacity), and Desaturation (Grayscale) for any overlay.
+ Advanced Coloring: Apply solid colors or Gradients (Vertical/Horizontal) to match your UI.
+ Positioning: Move overlays anywhere on your screen with independent X/Y offsets.
+ Dual Proc Support: Independently position "Left" and "Right" textures for abilities like Brain Freeze or Killing Machine.
+ Spec-Specific Profiles: Settings are saved per Specialization, so your Frost Mage and Holy Priest setups never conflict.
+ Preview: After getting a proc, there's a Preview button that works as a snapshot for better visibility on edits.
+ Border, Rotate, Mirror and Desaturate: Functions over the texture to edit to your liking.

**How to Use**

1. Open the Menu Type /soe in chat to open the configuration panel.

2. Select Your Target

   Class & Spec: The addon automatically detects your current spec, but you can manually configure others via the dropdown.
   
   Spell / Proc:
                Select "Global" to apply changes to ALL procs for that spec.
                Select a specific spell (e.g., Essence Burst) to override settings just for that ability.

4. Customize!

    Positioning: Use the sliders to move the overlay. If the spell has two parts (left/right), you can move them separately.
    Visuals: Change the blend mode to "Glow" (ADD) or "Normal" (BLEND), pick colors, or enable the new Pulse effect to make important procs stand out.

Slash Commands

    /soe - Opens the configuration menu.
